River-level forecasting with the TimesFM time-series foundation model; 20 hours/week

2022 — 2024

Undergraduate Research Fellow — CNPq / UFOP

Epidemiological processes in temporal mobility networks; 20 hours/week

  • Modeled disease propagation in metapopulation networks with an Eulerian SIR formulation and temporal population inflow/outflow tensors.
  • Studied synthetic mobility flows for the Belo Horizonte Metropolitan Region and 2022 IBGE census data, as well as Baidu mobility flows among 303 Chinese cities from January–February 2020; examined unrestricted and topology-constrained network scenarios.
